3. What the preview and planned service provide

The current preview lets you explore sample charts and comments, apply filters, and export matching sample rows as CSV. Entering a real video link still displays demo data. It does not fetch that video's comments or create a live sentiment analysis.

The planned live version will analyze up to 1,000 latest public top-level comments for a selected public video. This sample excludes replies and may not represent the entire audience. Results can be cached, partial, empty, or unavailable. The current preview has no billing or purchase features; any paid feature would need clear pricing and terms before you choose it.

4. How to interpret sentiment results

A sentiment label is an estimate about a comment's text. Sarcasm, mixed opinions, unfamiliar languages, and unknown vocabulary can produce mistakes or skipped comments. Read the original context before relying on a label. Model confidence is a class score, not a guarantee that a prediction is correct. Evaluation results from a training dataset do not establish accuracy for your selected video.
