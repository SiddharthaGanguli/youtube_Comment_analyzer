3. What demo and connected builds provide

Demo and synthetic test modes let you explore sample charts and comments, apply filters, and export matching sample rows as CSV. In those modes, entering a real video link still displays test data. It does not fetch that video's comments or create a live sentiment analysis.

A configured, connected service can analyze up to 1,000 latest public top-level comments for a selected public video when live fetching is enabled. This sample excludes replies and may not represent the entire audience. Results can be cached, partial, empty, or unavailable. Account sign-in uses Amazon Cognito; the website keeps its access-token session in this tab's session storage, while the connected extension uses trusted Chrome session storage. Website reloads and navigation within the same tab retain sign-in until the original access-token expiry or Sign out. Browsing does not extend that expiry. Signing out clears the app's local session and does not close your account or necessarily clear the provider's sign-in cookies.

Comment Lens has no billing or purchase features; any paid feature would need clear pricing and terms before you choose it.

4. How to interpret sentiment results

A sentiment label is an estimate about a comment's text. Sarcasm, mixed opinions, unfamiliar languages, and unknown vocabulary can produce mistakes or skipped comments. Read the original context before relying on a label. Model confidence is a class score, not a guarantee that a prediction is correct. Evaluation results from a training dataset do not establish accuracy for your selected video.
